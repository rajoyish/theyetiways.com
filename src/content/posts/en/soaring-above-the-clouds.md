Morning in our mountain lodge. Pine smoke rose from the hearth, frosted panes
looked out on snowy ridges, and our travel trunk sat on the timber floor like a
stubborn yellow boulder.

Inside that wicker trunk was everything three Yetis needed for the high peaks:
wool blankets, warm scarves, and crisp mountain apples. Shutting it was another
matter.

## Hopping on the Trunk

Babu Yeti did not hesitate. With a furrowed brow and a grunt of effort, he hurled
his mint-green weight onto the bulging lid. The trunk creaked. The wicker bowed.
The brass latch remained stubbornly apart. Beside him, our two companion
cockatiels fluttered in panic, chirping as Babu Yeti bounced again without
gaining an inch.

Mama Yeti watched from the hearth with an amused eyebrow. I stepped forward,
placed both sapphire-blue paws over the lid, and leaned down with my full bulk.
With a crisp metallic click, the brass latch locked into place.

Babu Yeti sat atop the closed trunk with a cheerful smile, nuzzling my cheek in
relief. I hoisted the trunk onto my shoulder while Babu Yeti grabbed his small
basket, the cockatiels perching on the handle. As we headed toward the doorway, I
looked back at our warm hearth. The heaviest luggage becomes weightless when
everyone lends a hand, but the memories you pack together will last a lifetime.

## Tea in the Open Sky

Past the gate, our wooden sky-glider waited on the rail. We climbed aboard, and
the rocky pass dropped away into an endless rolling sea of silver-white clouds.

Wisps of mist trailed off the wingtips as the glider cruised into clear sky. Babu
Yeti leaned against the railing, eyes wide with wonder, while Mama Yeti poured
warm tea into carved wooden cups. The cockatiels perched on the armrest, sipping
water from a wooden dish. Soaring above life's clouds is not about escaping the
world. It is about discovering new horizons side by side with the ones you love.

By nightfall, we reached the summit sanctuary, where lanterns illuminated tiered
pagodas beneath the stars. Babu Yeti and I turned toward each other with wide
grins, bringing our paws together in a crisp high-five that rang out across the
quiet peaks. Mama Yeti clapped in joy as our two feathered companions took
flight, sweeping around the monument's golden flame beacon.

> No summit is reached alone. The brightest destinations are the ones celebrated
with a shared high-five and open hearts.

## The Yeti Way

**The greatest height of an adventure is never the altitude, but the ones
sharing every breath of the climb.**

It is easy to imagine travel is about conquering a distant peak or proving how
much weight your shoulders can carry. In the high country, you quickly learn
that sheer muscle cannot fasten a stubborn latch, and soaring above clouds feels
empty without familiar paws beside you on the deck.

We carry what we need, lean on each other when the load is heavy, and leave room
for wonder along the way.

Pack with love. Fly together. High-five at the top. ❄️❤️
