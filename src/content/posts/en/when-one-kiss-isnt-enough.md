Snow was falling past the window, the hearth was crackling low, and the candle
on the sill had the whole room glowing amber. I was tucked against the rose
cushion on our olive-green sofa. My husband was on the other end, sinking
slowly into the knitted throw like a boulder settling into a riverbed.

He leaned over and gave me one little kiss on the cheek. Then he leaned back,
hummed a happy sleepy hum, and closed his eyes.

## One is a start

I opened my eyes and felt my cheeks go pinker than my fur. One kiss. Lovely.
A very good opening.

So I leaned over and kissed him back. A tiny soft smack on his blue cheek.
Then I sat back with my hands in my lap and my eyebrows up, smiling, waiting
for the next part.

There was no next part. Papa Yeti smiled in his sleep and did not move a
single strand of fur.

I gave him one more, just to be fair. Smack. Nothing. The fire popped. The wind
leaned on the window. Papa Yeti breathed slow and deep, like a Yeti whose day
was finished and whose wife was only getting started.

So I folded my arms.

## The pout

I want it on record that it was a big pout. Arms crossed high, cheeks hot,
mouth turned all the way down. The hearth seemed to sink lower out of
sympathy. Over in the wicker basket, Pebble Hedgehog peeked over the rim with
its nose twitching, the way you watch weather coming over a pass.

Then Papa Yeti opened his eyes. He turned his head, saw my face, and gasped.
I watched him understand everything at once. His eyebrows went up, then the
whole of him folded into the most sorry face on the mountain.

He did not explain. He did not ask what was wrong. He wrapped one long arm
around my shoulders and covered my cheek in quick little kisses, one after
another, until my arms gave up and unfolded on their own and I was laughing
with my eyes shut.

> One kiss tells me you love me. The second one tells me you noticed I was still there.

Tofu Cat blinked slowly at us from the windowsill, very satisfied. Then Babu
Yeti padded in from the hall rubbing one eye, took one look at the sofa, and
climbed right into the middle. Papa Yeti and I each put an arm around him,
and the fire came back up warm.

## The Yeti Way

**Love is not a box you tick once. It is a fire you keep feeding.**

Papa Yeti meant that first kiss with his whole heart. I know he did. But one
kiss on the way to sleep can feel like a door closing, even when it was meant
as a door opening.

And I could have sulked all night. The pout was not the point. The point was
that he woke up, looked, and answered without making me spell it out.

Give one more than you think is enough. Nobody ever complained. ❄️❤️
