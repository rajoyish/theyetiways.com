The cave was glowing with the soft light of the hearth fire. Papa Yeti had been up before the sun cleared the ridge, setting small ice crystals into the frost on the walls to catch the warmth of the room perfectly. A birthday is a moment to stop everything else and look closely at the path we have walked together.

Babu Yeti was bouncing on his toes near the cave entrance, trying to keep his green fur from bristling with pure excitement. I was holding a beautifully carved ice bowl filled with brightly glowing moss. The soft light painted Papa Yeti's bright blue fur in gentle golden tones as he stood in the center of the decorated cave. He looked over at me with soft, warm eyes. He always knows when a moment is ready to begin. He opened his wide arms, ready to welcome a brand new year.

## A Song for the Journey

Papa Yeti started the song with a wide, cheerful toothy smile. His deep voice filled the cavern, wishing a happy birthday and a blessed journey. I stepped forward to join him under the warm hearth glow. I sang my part of the wish, putting my whole heart into the hope that every dream would come true in the new year.

This is the truest gift we know how to give each other. True joy isn't found in grand spectacles, but in the warmth of home, the strength of family, and the love that guides your way. We sang together, our voices blending effortlessly. We lifted the glowing moss bowl higher, letting its light shine outward. May bright smiles light every step of your journey, and may every tomorrow bring you closer to all your dreams.

## Gathering the Light

Babu Yeti could not wait his turn any longer. He popped up between us with wide, innocent eyes and a cute playful smile, clapping his small paws together in absolute delight. Papa Yeti and I looked down at him, our hearts swelling with pride. Papa Yeti finished the song, singing about finding love and happiness on your very best day.

> "Another year is a fresh horizon waiting to be explored. Walk into it with hope in your heart, courage in your spirit, and reasons to celebrate every single day."

We raised our paws together in a unified cheer. The cave was filled with light, music, and happiness. The ice crystals sparkled brightly on the stone walls, but the real warmth was held safely between the three of us.

## The Yeti Way

**A birthday is not just a marker of time passed, but a gathering of light for the year ahead.**

When we celebrate someone we love, we are doing much more than singing a familiar song. We are pouring our hope and warmth into a single, shining moment. The world outside the cave can be cold and steep, but the love of your family is the fire you can carry with you through all of it.

Papa Yeti, Babu Yeti, and I do not have much use for complicated gifts. The best thing we can give is our presence, our unwavering support, and our voices raised together. That is the light that lasts long after the glowing moss fades.

May your heart always find its way back to the warm. ❄️❤️
