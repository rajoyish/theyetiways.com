The bass was doing that thing where you feel it in the floor of the cave before
you hear it. Babu Yeti had lit the good ice crystals, the ones that glow blue,
and we had turned a perfectly reasonable evening into the opposite of one.

I was not dancing well. I want that noted. I was attempting a twist no Yeti my
size should attempt, and a leap that knocked snow off the ledges, and Babu Yeti
was stomping along beside me copying every terrible move like it was
choreography we had rehearsed.

## Then the whole cave stopped

Babu Yeti went stiff first.

I came down out of a jump with one arm still in the air, turned, and there was my
wife at the mouth of the cave. Perfectly still. Snow on her shoulders. Saying
nothing at all.

The music kept playing. We were the ones who scratched to a halt. A gust of wind
came through the entrance and I heard every second of it, which tells you exactly
how quiet a den full of dance music can get.

So I stood there. Arm up. Caught.

## What I was actually waiting for

Permission. That is the honest answer, and it took me the rest of the night to
admit it.

Somewhere along the way I had decided that one of us needed to be the grown-up
who sighs at the noise, and I handed her that job without ever asking whether she
wanted it. Then I flinched from the version of her I had invented.

Mama Yeti grinned. Both arms went straight up, and she came into the middle of
the cave doing a shimmy that made Babu Yeti shriek.

> I was never afraid of being caught dancing. I was afraid of being the only one
> still willing to.

The music came back at full volume. Three of us stomping, clapping, snow coming
off the rocks in sheets, a small green Yeti bouncing between us like weather.

## The Yeti Way

**The one who loves you is not your audience. They are waiting for you to leave
room.**

I spent four seconds being embarrassed in front of somebody who has watched me
sleep with my mouth open through an entire monsoon. Four seconds is nothing. It
was still four seconds I gave away for no reason.

And Babu Yeti did not freeze because he was embarrassed. He froze because I did.
That is the part that stays with me. A child reads the room off your face, and my
face said we have been caught at something.

Nobody in this cave was ever going to stop the music. One of us just had to keep
going long enough for the rest to catch up.

Turn it up. Then make space. ❄️❤️
