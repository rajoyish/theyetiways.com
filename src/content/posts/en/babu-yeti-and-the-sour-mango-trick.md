The mango tree in the green valley below the snow is the best tree I know. The
mangoes hang gold and heavy, and the whole clearing smells like summer. Every
single one of them belonged to Kobi Squirrel. He told everybody so.

Pied Goat stretched up on its back legs under the lowest branch. The three
Woolly Hares hopped as high as hares can hop. Nobody reached anything. Kobi
Squirrel hugged a cluster to his tummy and said, "Shoo! They're all mine!"

I looked at the hares. I looked at the tree. Then I had an idea.

## The worst faces in the valley

Behind the mossy boulder I whispered the plan to Papa Yeti. He did not need to
understand it. He only needed to agree with me, loudly, about everything. He
gave me a thumbs-up so big it was almost a plan of its own.

Then I marched up to the tree and called, "Kobi! How can you eat such sour
mangoes?"

Kobi Squirrel nearly dropped his lunch. "Sour? These are sweeter than honey!"
So I told him to toss one down and prove it. He did. I took a big bite, and it
was the best mango of my life, and I made a face like I had licked a yak's
hoof. "Phooey! Sour beyond belief!"

Papa Yeti tasted it next. He shuddered from his tuft to his belly and groaned,
"It's practically mouth-puckering!" Papa Yeti is a terrible actor. Kobi
Squirrel believed every bit of it.

Mango after mango came tumbling down. We grimaced at all of them. Papa Yeti
winked at me once, and I had to hold my giggle in with both hands.

## The tree went bare

Then Kobi Squirrel lost his temper. He stamped, he puffed, and he shook every
branch until the last mango thumped into the soft grass. "I'm off to find a
sweeter tree!" he huffed, and he spread his flaps and glided into the jungle.

It worked. I called the goat and the hares out of the ferns, and there were
mangoes for everyone. That should have been the end.

Then Mama Yeti stepped into the clearing, looked at the heaps, and asked the
one question my plan had skipped.

> "And what about Kobi? He'll be hungry too."

My mango felt heavy after that. In the golden afternoon Kobi Squirrel crept
back out of the vines with his ears down, hungry and very quiet. I gave him the
biggest mango I had. He said thank you. Then he said sorry, which I did not
expect, and which I think was harder.

## The Yeti Way

**Mangoes taste sweetest when shared, even with the one who would not share.**

The trick got the mangoes down. It was a fib, though, and a fib only moves
fruit. It does not change anybody.

What changed Kobi Squirrel was coming back hungry and finding a mango waiting
for him. Nobody shooed him. Nobody pulled a face.

We ate together under the tree until the shadows grew long. Kobi Squirrel sat
next to me. Papa Yeti is still a terrible actor. ❄️❤️
