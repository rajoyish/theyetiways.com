The morning sun was just starting to burn through the mist rising off the gorge. Mama Yeti, Babu Yeti, our little red panda apprentice, and I were standing on the wet bedrock. It was a perfect, quiet start to the day. Then the quiet was broken. Six agile students in dark grey tunics leaped across the slippery rocks with quick footwork and surrounded us in combat stances. 

They were fast, and they thought we would fight back with fists. Mama Yeti moved first. She dropped low on the slick stone, coiled her legs beneath her, and launched straight up into the misty air. She spun elegantly past their reaching hands, then landed gracefully beside Babu Yeti to shield him. It was my turn to resolve the situation. 

## A gentle thunder

I stepped forward into the rushing river shallows to confront the next wave of students. I did not want to hurt them, but I needed to make a firm point. Gathering my strength, I leaped into the air above the shallow stream. 

I dropped down with all my weight, slamming my massive paws against the flooded bedrock. The thunderous crack echoed through the entire river gorge. An immense geyser of white water erupted outward from my paws. The powerful concussive surge swept rapidly across the stone plateau. 

It caught the six challengers right at the ankles. They lost their footing entirely, scrambling and slipping comically into the shallow runoff puddles. They splashed around on their hands and knees on the wet moss, completely bewildered by the wave. 

## The bow

When the drenched students finally scrambled to their feet, they were soaked, shivering, and looking very surprised. They realized instantly that they could not break our family defense. Our little red panda apprentice proudly hoisted his carved bamboo staff beside Babu Yeti, who was now beaming up at us with a relieved smile. 

> "Like water, the greatest power does not destroy its obstacles; it gently washes them away to leave behind mutual respect."

The challengers exchanged wide-eyed glances, understanding the lesson we had just delivered. They pressed their palms together at chest height and bowed deeply in sincere martial respect. Then they turned and retreated gracefully across the river rocks, disappearing into the mist. We lowered our guard, letting the sun dry our soft fur.

## The Yeti Way

**True strength isn't found in the blows we strike, but in the peace we choose to protect.**

When a family stands united in courage, they can calm the fiercest storm and turn any conflict into harmony. We could have fought those students with our bare hands. Instead, we used the mountain itself to show them that violence always breaks against a solid, unmoving foundation. 

You do not have to fight every single battle you are invited to. Sometimes the best defense is simply refusing to be moved, standing your ground, and letting the rushing river do the rest of the work. 

Stay peaceful, stay strong. ❄️❤️
