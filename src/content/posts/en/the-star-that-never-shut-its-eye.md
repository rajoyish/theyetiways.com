The sun had already left the ridge, but the sky had not been told yet. It was
still rose at the bottom and blue at the top, and the snow was doing that quiet
silver thing it does when nobody is watching. Then one star came out. Just one.

I stood by the snowbank with my head tilted, which is my thinking position. The
star was very small and very far up, higher than the tallest peak, and it
sparkled like it was saying something in a language I have not learned yet. I
wanted to know what it was. Nothing up there was answering.

## The traveler with the dark lantern

The rose went out of the sky. The whole valley turned the deep blue of the
inside of a cooking pot. I reached one hand up at the star, the way you reach
for a jar on a high shelf, and Mama Yeti came and slipped her hand under mine
instead. That was fine. That was better, honestly.

Below the ridge, a trail runs through the pines. Old Herb-Gatherer was walking
it, crunch, crunch, his reed lantern swinging at his side with a small creak.
The lantern was not lit. I checked twice.

He did not need it. The star had laid a thin silver line along the whole curve
of the path. He stopped once, put a hand on his chest, and looked straight up at
it with the face you make when somebody passes you the last momo.

## I did not shut my eye either

That night the curtains in my room were open, and the star was right there in
the window, resting above the sill like it had followed me home. I was supposed
to be asleep. I was doing research.

It never went off. The fleece rustled every time I sat up to check, and every
time I checked, it was still on duty.

> It did not know Old Herb-Gatherer was down there, and it lit his whole way home anyway.

Then it was morning, which happened while I was not looking, which is rude.
Mama Yeti was at the window beside me and the sun was sitting on the ridge. The
star had stayed up exactly until it was not needed. Then it let the sun take
the next shift.

The next night Papa Yeti came to the sill too, and the three of us stood there
while it came back on time like nothing special had happened.

## The Yeti Way

**You do not have to be big to be someone's way home. You only have to stay
lit.**

That star is about the size of nothing. It cannot see the trail. It has never
met Old Herb-Gatherer and never will. It just keeps shining in the same place,
night after night, and a whole path comes out of the dark because of it.

I still do not know what it is. I have decided that is allowed. Old
Herb-Gatherer did not know either, and he was grateful anyway.

Most nights I am the smallest one in the cave. I keep my light on anyway. ❄️❤️
