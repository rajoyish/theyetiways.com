I've been the new kid three times now. I'm not saying I'm good at it. I'm saying
I've collected data.

## Days one through three are just weather

The first three days feel terrible and that is normal. It's not a sign the year
will be bad. It's just what the start feels like when you don't know where the
bathrooms are yet. You wait it out like rain.

## What actually helped

- **Finding one person, not ten.** I stopped trying to be liked by the whole
  class. I found one Yeti who also didn't have anyone to sit with, and we sat
  together. That was enough to build on.
- **Saying yes to the boring invite.** Someone asked if I wanted to help stack
  chairs. I did not want to help stack chairs. I helped stack chairs. That's how
  I met two of my friends now.
- **Telling Mama the real version at night.** Not the "it was fine" version. The
  actual version. It didn't fix it but it made it lighter.

## The thing nobody tells you

By Friday, the new school wasn't "the new school" anymore. It was just school.
The scary part has an expiration date. You just can't see it from Monday.

If you're the new kid right now: it gets normal. Give it a week. Find your one
person. Stack the chairs.
